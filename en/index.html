Ponpon

The books you read, and the lines that stayed with you.

Ponpon is a reading tracker and a commonplace book for iPhone. Keep your shelf — Want to Read, Tsundoku, Reading, Read, Want to Reread, Did Not Finish — and save the sentences that moved you, each one tied to its book. Scan a page to pick a line, and turn quotes into beautiful cards to share. No account, no ads.
