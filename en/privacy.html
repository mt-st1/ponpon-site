Ponpon

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 21, 2026

Ponpon (the "App") treats your records as yours. The developer of the App (the "Developer") does not collect your personal information. This page explains what information the App handles and where it goes.

1. Information the Developer collects

None. The App has no account registration, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

2. Information the App handles on your device

  • Your shelf and quotes (book details, shelves, ratings, reviews, saved quotes, notes) are stored on your device.
  • If you are signed in to iCloud, these records are also stored in your own private iCloud storage and synced across devices that use the same Apple Account. The Developer cannot read this storage. iCloud is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • The camera is used only to read book barcodes and to read the text on a page you want to quote. Images are processed on your device and are never saved or sent.
  • Photos access is used only to save images you create (add-only permission). The App never reads your photo library. A photo you pick to read a barcode or text is processed on your device and is never saved or sent.

3. Information sent to outside services

When you search for books or display covers, the App asks the services below directly from your device. Only the words you search for or the book's ISBN are sent. Each service handles connection logs (such as IP addresses) under its own policy.

  • Open Library (Internet Archive): search for books outside Japan, covers — Terms
  • NDL Search (National Diet Library, Japan): title and author search — Privacy Policy
  • openBD: ISBN search for Japanese books, covers — Terms

4. Purchases (Ponpon Pro)

Purchases and payments are handled by Apple's App Store. The Developer does not receive your name or payment details. Your purchase status comes to your device directly from the App Store.

5. Feedback emails

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply and to investigate problems, and for no other purpose.

6. Deleting your records

Deleting a book or quote in the App removes it from your device and from iCloud. To delete everything, delete the App, then go to iPhone Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage and delete the App's data.

7. Children

The App can be used by anyone. The Developer does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes to this policy

We will post changes on this page. If we significantly change how information is handled, we will also mention it in the App's update notes.
