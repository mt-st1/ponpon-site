Frequently asked questions

Will my records move to a new iPhone?

Yes. Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple Account on your new iPhone and install Ponpon — your records sync automatically. You can check sync status in Settings (the gear on the Today tab) → iCloud Sync.

My records aren't syncing

Make sure you're signed in to iCloud in iPhone Settings → your name, and that you have iCloud storage available. Syncing can take a few minutes.

Can I bring my books from Goodreads or StoryGraph?

Yes. Export your library as a CSV file from Goodreads (My Books → Import and export → Export Library) or StoryGraph (Manage Account → Export StoryGraph Library), then in Ponpon go to Settings → Import from another app and choose the file. Books already on your shelf are skipped.

I bought Ponpon Pro but it isn't active / I changed phones

Go to Settings → See Ponpon Pro → Restore purchases. You need to be signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you purchased with.

How do I cancel Ponpon Pro?

Go to iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Ponpon Pro. You can keep using Pro until the end of the period. If you cancel during the free trial, you won't be charged. The lifetime purchase has nothing to cancel.

What's free and what's Pro?

Your shelf, quotes, streaks and widgets, iCloud sync, and quote and top-picks cards are free forever, and you can scan 5 pages a month for free. Pro adds unlimited page scans, yearly and monthly recaps (this month is free), reading stats and whole-shelf images, extra card paper colors, and removing the Ponpon stamp.

Some books have no cover

Covers come from Open Library and openBD. Books they don't have are shown with a cover designed from the title.

I can't find a book

Try the barcode on the back cover, or search with fewer words. Books outside Japan are searched in Open Library, a community-built catalog, so some recent titles may be missing. You can always add a book by hand with "Add manually".

How do I delete all my records?

See "Deleting your records" in the Privacy Policy.
