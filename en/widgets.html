Ponpon

Using widgets

Put Ponpon on your Home Screen or Lock Screen to see your streak and a quote from your past without opening the app.

Widget types

  • Streak (small, Lock Screen): how many days in a row you've saved a quote. Tap it to write today's quote.
  • A quote from your past (medium, large): one quote you saved before today, changing daily. The large size uses Japanese-style vertical text.

Add to your Home Screen

  1. Touch and hold an empty area of your Home Screen until the apps jiggle, then tap Edit → Add Widget.
  2. Find "Ponpon" in the list (or type Ponpon in the search field).
  3. Swipe to choose Streak or A quote from your past and a size, then tap Add Widget.
  4. Move it where you like and tap Done.

Add to your Lock Screen

  1. Touch and hold the Lock Screen, then tap Customize → Lock Screen.
  2. Tap the widget area below the time and choose Ponpon's Streak (circular, rectangular, or inline).
  3. Tap Done.

Troubleshooting

"A quote from your past" is empty

It picks from quotes saved before today, so it starts showing the day after you save your first quote.

The numbers or quote are out of date

Widgets update when you return to the Home Screen after adding or deleting a quote. If not, open Ponpon once and go back.

Ponpon doesn't appear in the widget list

Open Ponpon once and try again. If it still doesn't appear, restart your iPhone.
